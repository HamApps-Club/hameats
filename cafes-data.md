## Euphoria

"Coffee, bubble tea, and made-to-order lunch, as well as grab-and-go wrap and salad options." Open to the public. The site doesn't list prices. The few prices below come from photos of chalkboards posted in March 2024, so they may be out of date.

### Food
- **New York Bagels:** Plain, Everything, Asiago, Cheddar Jalapeño, Sesame, French Toast. Add-ons: butter, cream cheese, bacon.
- **Breakfast Sandwich:** seasoned eggs and cheese on any bagel; meat: bacon or sausage; sauce: chipotle, ketchup, hot sauce.
- **Mac & Cheese:** homemade, gluten free available. Add: chipotle, ketchup, hot sauce, BBQ sauce, bacon.
- **Hummus:** made in house, with tortilla chips, baby carrots or pretzel chips.
- **Daily Lunch Specials** (sandwiches, wraps, salads; rotating; GF available). Side: Lay's chips, apple, baby carrots or white cheddar puffs. Examples from 2024 boards: Honey Curry Chicken Salad Wrap $8.75; Asian Sesame Chicken Salad $8.25.
- **To-Go Cup:** lunch special or salad ingredients in a cup, "quick meal."
- **Muffins** (baked daily, rotating): Blueberry, Chocolate Chip, Cinnamon Crumble, Pistachio, Double Chocolate, Lemon Chia.
- **Euphoria Bars:** homemade granola bars.
- **Power Bites:** Peanut Butter (oats, peanut butter, flaxseed, honey, cacao chips); Almond Chia (oats, almond butter, chia, plant protein, honey, almonds, dried cherries).
- **Cookies:** baked daily; regular or gluten free.

### Drinks
Sizes: hot S 12 oz / M 16 oz / L 20 oz; iced M 16 oz / L 24 oz (unless noted).
- **Coffee:** medium or dark roast; hot or iced.
- **Espresso:** Double Shot, Cortado, Americano, Cappuccino.
- **Latte** (small hot is a flat white), **Chai Latte**, **London Fog** (steamed milk, vanilla, Earl Grey): hot or iced.
- **Hot Chocolate:** S, M or L.
- **Frappes** (iced coffee or milk base; M or L): Chocolate, Vanilla, Pistachio, Chai, Matcha.
- **All Natural Smoothies** (banana, yogurt, milk option; S 12 / M 16 / L 22 oz): Strawberry; Tropical Oasis (mango, OJ); Hamilton (acai, blueberry, raspberry, strawberry, blackberry); Coco Loco (pineapple, coconut); Four Berry Fusion. Base: whole, skim, almond, oat milk or OJ. Build your own, add 1–3 of: whey protein, plant protein, peanut butter, spinach, granola, almonds, chia, flax, energy shot. Popping boba or tapioca.
- **Bubble Tea** (traditional, blended or hot; M or L): Black Milk Tea, Matcha Green Tea, Taro, Thai Tea, Guava (seasonal). Popping boba or tapioca.
- **Lotus Energy** (all-natural purée with White Lotus and seltzer; M or L): Four Berry, Acai, Mango, Pineapple, Strawberry. Add-ons: popping boba, tapioca, extra Lotus shot. Price on a 2024 board: $4.75.
- **Tea:** Earl Grey Cream, African Nectar, Organic Breakfast, Organic Green Dragon, Marrakesh Mint, Chamomile Citrus.
- **Add-ons for coffee and lattes:** flavor shots (vanilla, caramel, hazelnut, honey, maple, toasted marshmallow, toffee crunch, white chocolate, SF vanilla, SF caramel); caramel or mocha sauce; extra espresso shot; oat milk; almond milk.
